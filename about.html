About Me

I am a data enthusiast with a passion for languages and a drive to innovate. I hold a master’s degree in Information Systems and Applications from National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan, where I merged my love for data science with my fascination for language and communication.

Throughout my professional journey, I have used data not only to anlyze problems but also to expand access and opportunities for communities. At Home Credit, my work helped provide financial access to the unbanked sector, giving people opportunities to participate in the formal economy. Later, at Kalibrr, I joined a startup with the mission to help small and medium-sized companies hire better and grow sustainably. I also worked with loan data supporting small to medium entrepreneurs, because I believe access to resources like credit can be a lifeline that allows people to build businesses, improve their livelihoods, and shape better futures. These experiences shaped my belief that data, when used ethically and strategically, can drive social impact.

My academic journey has been shaped by a variety of data science projects, particularly in Natural Language Processing (NLP). For my thesis, I explored how AI can enhance writing and communication, reinforcing my belief that data is more than numbers—it can transform industries, from detecting fraud and predicting diseases to optimizing business processes and improving education.
