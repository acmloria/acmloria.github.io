About Me

Greetings! I’m Angelica Cassandra Loria, a data enthusiast with a passion for languages and a drive to innovate. I hold a master’s degree in Information Systems and Applications from National Tsing Hua University in Taiwan, where I merged my love for data science with my fascination for language and communication.

During my time at NTHU, I was honored to receive the ICDF scholarship, which not only supported my studies but also immersed me in the vibrant world of Mandarin Chinese. What started as a requirement soon grew into a true passion, inspiring me to continue my Chinese studies. At the same time, I help Taiwanese learners improve their English skills. I see myself aligned with Taiwan’s Bilingual 2030 goals because I believe language opens doors to new perspectives, growth, and opportunities for people to reach their full potential.

My academic journey has been shaped by a variety of data science projects, particularly in Natural Language Processing (NLP). For my thesis, I explored how AI can enhance writing and communication, reinforcing my belief that data is more than numbers—it can transform industries, from detecting fraud and predicting diseases to optimizing business processes and improving education.
